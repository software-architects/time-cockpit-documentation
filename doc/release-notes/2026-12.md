# 📅 December 2026

## ✨ Improvements

- **Quick Picks and Dynamic Dates in Date Fields**  
  Below the calendar of a date field, quick picks set yesterday, today, or the start or end of the week, the month or last month with one click, and show the date each one stands for today. In the filter of a list they set a dynamic date: the field shows an fx icon, its tooltip names the rule and the date it gives today, and a list profile saves the rule, so a profile for last month stays current. **Use fixed date** turns a dynamic date into a fixed one, and **Custom rule** builds other dynamic dates such as "7 days before today". **Alt** + **Down** or **F4** opens the calendar. See [Dates in List Filters](~/doc/ui/lists/filter-dates.md).

- **Select All Rows of a Group**  
  In a grouped list, every header row has a checkbox that selects all rows of the group, also when the group is collapsed and including nested groups, without clearing the selection in other groups. A partly selected group shows a dash in its checkbox and the number of selected rows, for example **4/9**, in its header row. The menu of a header row offers **Select all ... items** and **Clear group selection** as well. See [Grouping Lists](~/doc/ui/lists/grouping.md#select-the-rows-of-a-group).

- **Pair sidekick in One Click**  
  Pairing time cockpit sidekick with the Time Sheet Calendar no longer means copying an address and a token. The right pane of the calendar guides you through it in two steps, installing and pairing: click **Pair with sidekick** there, then **Allow** in sidekick: sidekick asks in a small window in front, also when it runs only in the notification area or the menu bar, and the calendar picks up the token by itself. Each address of time cockpit is paired once. As long as no calendar is paired, **Open time cockpit** in the tab **Pairing** of sidekick opens the calendar, which then asks on its own. The right pane also offers **Pair with sidekick** when the token is missing or does not match, and the calendar finds sidekick by itself when it restarted on another port. The calendar settings show the state of the pairing and offer **Remove pairing**; if you do not want to use sidekick, **Hide the pane** at the bottom of the right pane hides it. The manual pairing with address and token stays available in the settings under **Pair manually with address and token**, for older versions of sidekick. See [Pair sidekick With the Calendar](~/doc/sidekick/installation-and-pairing.md#pair-sidekick-with-the-calendar).

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

- **Switching Between the Day, Work Week and Week View Sticks**  
  Switching the view of the time sheet calendar sometimes did not take: the toolbar did nothing, or the double-click on a day switched back on its own a moment later. A calendar that had once been too narrow for several days kept putting its own view back. The view you choose now stays. Where the calendar really is too narrow, one day at a time is all it shows: the week and work week views are greyed out and say why, and your chosen view comes back as soon as there is room for it. In the day heads of the week views, the deviation and the bar below it are no longer cut off in narrow columns. See [Time Sheet Calendar](~/doc/timesheet-calendar/calendar.md).

- **Lists That Load on Open Wait for Preset Filter Values**  
  A list that loads on open sometimes reported its filter as incomplete when a mandatory filter field selects its first entry by itself and that entry arrived late. The list now waits for such fields before it loads, and loads once a slow field has picked its value. A list that is not loaded now says why: it loads only on demand, its filter is incomplete or invalid, or checking the filter took too long. If checking the filter takes too long while the list already shows data, a notification says so; click **Apply Filter** to try again. Back-reference tabs on forms name the same reason in the tooltip of their row count.
